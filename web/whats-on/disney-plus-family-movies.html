LEGO Star Wars: The Mandalorian (2026)

TV-Y7 · Added September 2 · Best for 5–10

The brick special that landed with the movie. Recasts a lot of the live-action beats as slapstick. Pedro Pascal is not Din here — it’s a LEGO voice cast — which is the point. This is the Grogu watch if live Mandalorian still feels like a Western.

Watch for

  • Cartoon blasters and comic falls
  • Cameos of characters who die in the show, played for gags

Preview first

The Mandalorian and Grogu (2026)

PG · Added September 2 · Best for 8+

The May theatrical, now on the service that made the show. Same rules as the series: bounty-hunter violence, Grogu in danger, a Hutt plot. Cute baby, not a baby movie. If they watch The Mandalorian already, this is the obvious Friday night.

Watch for

  • Shootouts and creature attacks
  • Grogu in peril (the emotional hook)
  • Fantasy violence at a PG, not G, level

Already on — we rated these

  • Star Wars: The Clone Wars — 133 episodes we rated: 90% mild. Better weeknight TV than putting on Revenge of the Sith because the movie is ‘the one with the clones.’
  • Phineas and Ferb, Gravity Falls, Amphibia, The Owl House — All scored episode by episode. Gravity Falls and Owl House need a preview on the spookier eps.

On the service, not for family night

  • Andor, The Acolyte, adult Marvel — On the same app. Not family just because the logo is a mouse.

Coming later this month

If they already watch the show, probably. It is PG space-western violence, not a preschool Grogu cartoon. Use the LEGO special for under-8s.
