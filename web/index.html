Common questions

How do you decide if an episode is OK for kids?

Each episode is scored 1–5 for violence, sex and language from its transcript. The overall score is the highest of the three. 1–2 is mild, 3 is caution worth previewing, and 4–5 is too much for younger kids.

Do you show what actually happens in the episode?

Yes. Every episode page lists each flagged theme, how many times it occurs, and the moments behind it — direct quotes, or a short description of the scene when there is no line to quote.

Where should I start if I just want something safe tonight?

Open What to watch — each show has a list of the safest episodes and the ones to skip, built from the same 1–5 scores. For new movies on Netflix and other apps, see What's on.

What's on vs What to watch — what's the difference?

What's on is the weekly list of newly added family movies on Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Max, Apple TV+ and Paramount+. What to watch is episode-by-episode scores for shows in our catalog.

What from the 2026 Emmys is OK to watch with kids?

Most Primetime winners are adult TV. See Emmys 2026 with kids — The Muppet Show, Abbott Elementary, the Children's & Family Emmy winners — and why comedy winner Widow's Bay is a skip.
